Sound cues for theatre and live events

Scene banks, looping ambience and per-pad outputs, on a screen you can read from the back of the booth.

Download PadVibeSee all features

For small productions, schools, churches and events, a pad per cue is often all you need. PadVibe puts every cue on a clearly named, colour-coded pad, with a tab for each scene.

Route front-of-house sounds and on-stage monitors to different channels of a multi-channel interface, and keep ambience on a looping pad with its own fader.

Good to know. PadVibe has not been audited for safety-critical use. Always have a backup plan for live shows.

Tips from the way PadVibe works

  • Use the floating countdown timer to see how much of the playing cue remains.
  • Pads can be triggered from a MIDI controller, which many operators prefer to a mouse.
  • Move between scenes with tabs. Switching tabs stops running sounds, so the next scene starts clean.
  • PadVibe copies audio into its own library, so a pad keeps working if the original file moves.
  • Rehearse with your real audio interface connected: device names and channel counts come from the system.
FAQ

Common questions

Is PadVibe a cue-list sequencer?

No. It is a pad-based player: you trigger each cue yourself. There is no timeline or automatic follow-on cue.

How reliable is it?

The audio runs in a separate process that PadVibe restarts automatically if it stops responding, and detailed logs are kept. As with any live system, rehearse with your actual setup.

Can I use several audio outputs?

Yes. Each pad can use its own output device and, on multi-channel interfaces, its own channels.

Is it free for schools and churches?

Yes. PadVibe is free to download and use.
